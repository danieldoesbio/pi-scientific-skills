# Run notebook: sci_find ranking after the v2.72.0 sync

**Status: done (2026-10-05).** Offline only, on pi 1.0.0. No model in the loop.

## Why

The sync from upstream v2.69.0 (`49c6e97`) to v2.72.0 (`526ebce`) rewrote the
`description` of 138 of the 162 shared skills (mostly condensed, imperative to
third person) and added 14 skills (`fictiv`, the 15th, is excluded). `sci_find`
ranks over those descriptions and the SKILL.md bodies, so its development
numbers and its no-match rule were calibrated on a corpus that no longer
exists.

## The no-match rule

One `test-search.mjs` check failed after the sync: "read alignment" returned
nothing. Its best skill scored 1.47 (was 1.88), under `minTop` (2.5), and
0.301 of the most it could score (was 0.373), under `minShare` (0.35).

Shares on the 176-skill tree, short queries only (all 1,056 development
queries with the target in the top 8 pass on `minTop` alone):

| read alignment | must match | 1.47 | 0.301 |

| e-mail my landlord | must return nothing | 2.48 | 0.194 |

`minShare` moves to 0.28, between the two. The margin is thin on both sides,
and "e-mail my landlord" now sits 0.02 under `minTop`. The next sync should
re-run this table before trusting either constant.

The bm25f known miss "write the methods section of my paper" is now found at
#2 and is checked again, so `test-search.mjs` makes 107 ranking checks.

## Not re-run

  out of git and were not available to this run.
- **The probe and paraphrase sets do not cover the 14 new skills.** Their
  rows stop at the 162-skill catalogue, so the numbers above say nothing
  about whether `sci_find` finds `primer-design` or `flowkit`. Adding probes
  for them is open work.
- **`TOKENS_PER_SKILL`** was checked with the chars-per-token fallback only
  (tiktoken's encoding download was blocked): 138.1 tokens per skill against
  the constant's 143, a 3.4% drift, under the 10% tolerance. Left at 143.
